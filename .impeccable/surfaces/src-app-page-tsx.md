## Direction contract

THESIS: Each project is revealed like a product launch on a dark stage: one product lit, one sentence says what it is. It refuses the light grid of project cards and the glowing neon dev portfolio (no glows, no gradients on type).

OWN-WORLD: Black ground #0a0a0a everywhere, off-white type #f2f2ee, dim type at 62%. Neon green #39ff14 is the only colour and marks what is live: the strap, the badge card, the stage counter, primary buttons, link underlines, and the drenched close. Soft radial spotlight behind whatever is on stage. One family, Bricolage Grotesque: condensed 800 for names and titles, text widths for everything else, tabular figures for counters. No mono labels, no italic serif, no pills except on the badge. Illustrative product screens keep their own neutral hues.

STORY: The founder meets Filip and plays with the neon badge. Four segments show four real products with real or clearly labelled screens and one factual line each. "Also" and "Backstage" follow as quick facts. The close floods neon: one sentence, one black LinkedIn button.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top left: the one-line intro. Top right: "Product Developer", the origin line and a LinkedIn link underlined in neon. Neon badge centred on a neon V lanyard, its card overlapping FILIP STEFANOVSKI set edge to edge in off-white condensed Bricolage along the bottom; stacked FILIP / STEFANOVSKI on phones.

FORM: Keynote Stage, position 3 of 7 on the ordered list, seed key 0e6010e8.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
